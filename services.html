Services

Everything your song needs

Bring us one song or a whole record. We'll take your creative and artistic vision and help you realize it, whether you need the whole journey or just the last step.

●Recording

Bridge Recording started as a mobile studio, built so musicians could get studio-quality recordings without huge costs or hard-to-reach rooms. We're musicians too, so we know how to manage energy and catch the golden moments that can get lost in a sterile, traditional studio.

Vocals, instruments, full bands: we'll help you capture the take that matters.

≋Mixing

Mixing blends your recorded tracks into one finished song. We bring out the best in your multi-track recording by balancing levels and panning and shaping the space around each part with EQ, compression, reverb, delay and chorus.

When we're finished, you get a mixdown that's ready for mastering.

◆Mastering

Mastering lets you share your music the way it's meant to be heard, with a consistent, professional sound across devices and streaming platforms.

  • Careful level, EQ and compression to add polish and punch
  • Your songs sit well together across an album, with fades and spacing taken care of
  • Up to two revisions, so you get a master you're happy with
  • CD-quality WAV (16-bit, 44.1 kHz) and high-quality 320 kbps MP3 files

$100 per track

✦Production

Have songs but not the sound yet? We'll work with you on arrangements, parts and tones, from first demo to finished record, so the music ends up sounding like you meant it to.

We produce our own bands' records too, so we know the whole road from idea to release day.

Every project is different. Tell us about yours and we'll put together a quote.
